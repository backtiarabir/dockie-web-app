We're Students Too.

We saw a problem on campus and decided to fix it.

Our Story

It started during finals week. The library printers were jammed, the queue was out the door, and we had an assignment due in 15 minutes. We realized that while the official printers were broken, there were hundreds of personal printers sitting idle in dorm rooms just a few feet away.

Dockiee was built to connect those dots. To create a decentralized printing network where students help students.

Our Mission

Save Time

No more running across campus to find the one working operational printer.

Empower Peers

Putting money back into the pockets of students, not big corporations.

Trust & Safety

We take security seriously. All Printer Owners are verified students with valid university IDs. Files are encrypted during transfer and automatically wiped from the printer's queue after pickup. We are building a community based on accountability and mutual respect.
